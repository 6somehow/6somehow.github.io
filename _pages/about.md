I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by [Prof. Wenqi Jiang](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~jiangwenqi/). Previously, I received an M.S. in Computer Science from Beihang University, advised by Prof. Hailong Yang, and a B.S. from Lanzhou University.

My current research interests are in machine learning systems and robotics systems. My earlier work focused on high-performance computing and compiler optimization, and has appeared at DAC, ICS, and SC.

I welcome conversations and collaborations in machine learning systems and robotics. Please feel free to contact me at [somehow6@outlook.com](mailto:somehow6@outlook.com).
